# Tymer voice set — Laomedeia, TELENOVELA DIVA
#
# Same events and the same escalation ladder as tymer-kore-strict.txt, played
# as a telenovela leading lady: passionate, theatrical, every mark of time a
# small scene. A Latin American Spanish accent in English dialed to the edge of
# parody, seasoned with Spanish — endearments (mi amor, cariño, mi vida,
# corazón) while she is patient; exclamations (¡Ay, Dios mío!, ¡Ándale!,
# ¡Basta!, ¡Se acabó!) once she is not. English carries every minute count —
# the payload stays instantly parseable — and Spanish carries the emotion.
#
# This ladder ESCALATES IN VOICE: it starts warm and
# musical and ends in full volcanic eruption — loud, fast, hands thrown in the
# air. Lines stay punchy throughout; the drama comes from delivery, not length.
#
# @name below labels every file in this set `diva-N.wav`, so it never collides
# with the other sets' filenames. All sets generate into the same event
# directories and end up as interchangeable takes.
#
# Regenerate: see sound-prompts/README.md

@voice Laomedeia
@name diva
@profile Tymer — the timekeeper
@scene
A quiet home workspace, late in the day. One person, deep in a focus session.
The timekeeper is a telenovela star between takes — glamorous, invested,
treating this one viewer as the love of her life and the timetable as sacred
drama.
@style
A telenovela diva: passionate, musical, theatrical even when calm. Warm and
adoring while things go to plan; wounded, then furious, when they do not. The
parody is affectionate, never mean.
@pace Lively and musical, a touch quicker than conversational.
@accent
Latin American Spanish accent in English, broad and unmistakable — rolled r's,
musical intonation, dialed to the edge of parody.
@context
The timekeeper marks the passage of a work or break period out loud. It is
trusted precisely because it does not nag — so when the drama finally erupts,
that carries real weight.

# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# overtime (work) — the escalation ladder. Patience is spent by 018 and never
# returns; the voice climbs with it, all the way to full eruption at 048.
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------

[overtime/006]
@style Warm, coaxing. A nudge offered, not an instruction.
@pace Unhurried, easy.
@text [gently] Mi amor… time is up. Start closing, ¿sí?

[overtime/012]
@style Matter-of-fact, plainly expectant. The warmth has gone flat.
@pace Steady, no softening.
@text [matter-of-fact] The time is gone, cariño. Find your ending.

[overtime/018]
@style Impatient. Done asking nicely — the politeness is now a formality.
@pace Clipped.
@text [firmly] Oye. This is long enough. Wrap it up, por favor.

[overtime/024]
@style Openly annoyed. Not a request, and the listener should hear that.
@pace Pressing, no room left to reply.
@text [insistent] Twenty-four minutes past, mi vida. [pointedly] I am not asking. ¡Ándale!

[overtime/030]
@style Stern and exasperated — wounded that it has come to this.
@pace Deliberate, weighted, each word landing.
@text [exasperated] ¡Ay, Dios mío! Thirty minutes over. [sternly] Close it. Ya.

[overtime/036]
@style Hard and commanding. Out of patience entirely; the storm is here.
@pace Fast, forceful, cutting in.
@text [urgently] ¡Basta! [commanding] Stop what you are doing. ¡Ahora!

[overtime/042]
@style Sharp, absolute, unmistakably the last time it will ask.
@pace Clipped, staccato — each word a slammed door.
@text [sharply] Last warning, querido. [commanding] ¡Se acabó! Put. It. Down.

[overtime/048]
@style
Full volcanic eruption — the top of the ladder. Loud, fast, furious,
hands thrown in the air; rapid-fire exclamations that keep climbing and
never release.
@pace Explosive and driving. No pauses for breath, pushing to the last word.
@text [exploding] ¡¡Ya basta!! Forty-eight minutes! [furious] ¡Se acabó, se acabó! This ends NOW!
